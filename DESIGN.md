# Centscan design direction

Brand source of truth: [brand/BRAND.md](brand/BRAND.md). Tokens: [src/styles/tokens.css](src/styles/tokens.css) (generated by motionui `system`: accent `#0B6E4F`, warm neutrals, ratio 1.333, comfortable, sharp radius; every contrast pair measured and passing). Use token roles only: never type a hex, pixel size or shadow by hand.

## Design read
- **Subject and audience:** a measuring instrument for Arc contracts. Its vocabulary is receipts, ledgers, till rolls, rubber stamps, gas meters and engineering manuals. The readers are Solidity builders, grant reviewers and finance people who want numbers, not adjectives.
- **Surface modes:** `/` is **Persuade**: an animated product page that ends in "Launch app". `/app` and `/c/[address]` are **Operate**: a scanner and its report.
- **Platform:** web, from 360 px to wide desktop, keyboard first.

|---|---|---|---|
| Site `/` | 7 | 7 | 3 |
| App `/app`, `/c/*` | 3 | 3 | 6 |

## Seed (adapted from the motionui deal)
Seed 1: an Ulm School product manual, uncoated paper and ink, two-ink print, a modular grid with one deliberate break per screen, snappy short springs, and 2.5D CSS layers with no WebGL. We keep it and translate it:
- **The manual:** sections are numbered figures (Fig. 1, Fig. 2), with captions set like a Braun or Ulm operating manual. Diagrams are drawn with hairline rules, not illustrations.
- **Two-ink print:** **ink** (`--text`) and **ledger green** (`--accent`) on receipt paper (`--page`). Where they overprint (stamps, the total line), use `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so green over ink reads darker, like real overprinted ink. There is no third colour; amber and red are status signals only.
- **Extended display face:** the brand fixes IBM Plex, so display type is Plex Sans 600 at `--text-5xl` with tight tracking (`--tracking-5xl`). Every number, address and hash is set in Plex Mono with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- **2.5D:** the receipt leaves a printer slot with a slight perspective curl (`perspective` + `rotateX` on transform only).

**Anti-reference:** the crypto-dashboard cliché. That means dark mode, neon, glassmorphism, gradients, glowing orbs, three identical feature cards, and "Unlock seamless web3" copy.

## Palette proportions
70% paper (`--page`, `--surface`), 22% ink (`--text`, `--text-muted`, rules), 7% ledger green (`--accent`, `--accent-text`, `--accent-subtle`), 1% signal (`--warning`, `--danger`). One primary action per view.

## Type
- Plex Sans 400 and 600 only, at most two weights per screen.
- Plex Mono for data.
- Scale from the tokens: `--text-xs` … `--text-5xl`. Lines stay within `--measure`.
- Manual captions use `--text-sm` in `--text-muted`, prefixed "Fig. N".

## Layout
12-column modular grid, gutter `--gutter`, section rhythm `--section`. Hairline rules (`--divider`) instead of boxes; cards only where something is truly an object (the receipt, the badge).

## Signature moment
**The till.** In the site hero, a printer slot feeds out a receipt as you scroll. GSAP ScrollTrigger pins the hero and scrubs the paper's `translateY`, and line items land one by one, using real numbers from the newest cached report. Then the total line prints, and a green **ATTESTED ON ARC** rubber stamp presses down: a short spring on `scale` and `rotate` with multiply blend. With no cached report, the receipt prints the method instead (the formula lines), never invented numbers. The app reuses the same receipt object, so the report you build looks like the one on the site.

Everything else stays quiet.

## Motion language
- **Easing:** `--ease-out` for entrances; short springs (stiffness 400–600, damping 30–40) for presses and the stamp.
- **Durations:** `--duration-fast` for feedback (≤200 ms), `--duration-base` for state changes, `--duration-story` only for the scroll story.
- **Site:**
  - GSAP + ScrollTrigger for the pinned hero scrub and figure reveals: hairline rules draw with `scaleX`, captions offset by 40 ms.
  - Framer Motion for component press/hover.
- **App:**
  - Framer Motion only: receipt lines print with a 40 ms stagger when a fresh report completes; the tab indicator uses a layout animation; the progress lines print as steps arrive.
- **Never:**
  - Animate the LCP element (the hero H1, and the receipt's first frame is rendered in place).
  - Fade-up every section.
  - Animate anything except transform and opacity.
  - Put a hidden start state in CSS: `gsap.from` and Framer `initial` write it at runtime.
- **Reduced motion:** no ScrollTrigger registration, receipt fully printed and static, stamp already down.
- **Bundle:** gsap 3.15 core + ScrollTrigger ≈ 45 kB gzip on `/` only; framer-motion is already in the app.

## 3D and imagery
2.5D CSS only (perspective curl of the paper, layered slot). No WebGL, no stock imagery, no illustrations of people. Diagrams are inline SVG with hairline strokes in `--text`, and `--accent` for the measured path.

## Voice
Plain, exact, numerate (see BRAND.md Voice).
- **Hero:** "On Arc, gas is a price." / "Centscan reads real transaction receipts and tells you what each function of a contract costs to call, in cents."
- **Empty (app):** "No contracts scanned yet. Paste an address to print the first receipt."
- **Error:** what failed, plus the next step: "Arc RPC did not answer in time. Try again in a minute."

No ALL-CAPS eyebrows except the rubber stamp (a real stamp is caps). No "→" on every button. No made-up testimonials, logos or user counts.

# v2 direction (14 Oct 2026, after review)

The user's critique of v1: space is wasted, the type lacks punch, the background is flat, the motion is too quiet, and the site feels slow. Their decisions: a **3D paper till** hero, **Plex Sans Condensed** for display, and **light paper with rich texture**. These override v1 where they conflict.

## Changes to the dials
| Surface | Variance | Motion | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site `/` | 8 | 9 | 5 |
| App `/app`, `/c/*` | 4 | 5 | 7 |

## Space
- Use the width: a fluid 12-column grid up to 1600 px, with content filling it. Hero type spans 7–8 columns, and the 3D till owns the rest.
- No narrow centred column with empty margins on desktop. Sections alternate full-bleed bands, such as the dark ink formula band (below).
- In the app, the report uses a 3-zone layout on wide screens: the receipt, the cost table and side panels. `/app` puts the input and recent scans side by side.

## Type
- **Display:** IBM Plex Sans Condensed 600, loaded with next/font as `--font-plex-condensed`, at huge sizes. The hero uses `clamp()` from the token scale, up to about 9rem on wide screens. Display tracking is tight, and display size is set on condensed only.
- **Text:** Plex Sans 400/600. **Data:** Plex Mono with tabular numbers.
- Big numbers (cents) are set in Plex Mono 600 at display size: the price is the hero of every receipt.

## Background
- **Paper:** warm `--page` plus a fixed full-viewport grain layer (a tiny tiled noise texture as an SVG `feTurbulence` data URI at about 4% opacity), and faint ledger rules (`--divider`) on the hero.
- **The one full-bleed contrast band:** the formula is set on ink (`--text` as background, `--page` text, green-on-ink accents per BRAND `--green-on-ink`).
- **Not allowed:** gradient blobs or glows.

## Signature: the 3D paper till (three.js)
- **Scene:** a printer body (simple extruded rounded box in ink) with a slot. A **receipt ribbon** is a subdivided `PlaneGeometry` (about 60×200 segments) with a vertex shader. A curl and droop are driven by a `uFeed` uniform, so paper leaving the slot bends under gravity and sways slightly with the pointer.
- **Paper material:** fragment shader with paper grain (value noise), subtle fibres and a soft edge shadow from the curl normal. Real receipt text is drawn into a `CanvasTexture` from the newest cached report: Plex Mono, same lines as the v1 receipt, and the green total.
- **Scroll (GSAP ScrollTrigger scrub):**
  - `uFeed` goes from 0 to 1, so the paper feeds out and the lines appear.
  - At 0.9 the **ATTESTED ON ARC** stamp, a green decal plane, slams down with a spring, but only if the report is attested. Otherwise it prints "Attest on Arc: one click in the app".
- **Pointer:** gentle parallax tilt of the camera (±4°) and a paper sway.
- **Lighting:** one warm key light, a soft fill and a contact shadow plane. Light background; the paper is the brightest object.
- **Performance contract:**
  - The canvas mounts after first paint via dynamic `import("three")` inside `useEffect`. The H1 (LCP) is plain HTML and never waits for or depends on WebGL.
  - DPR capped at 1.75.
  - Render only while the hero is on screen (IntersectionObserver) and while something changes.
  - Pause on `visibilitychange`.
  - Dispose geometry, materials, textures and the renderer on unmount.
  - Fallback: if WebGL is unavailable or `prefers-reduced-motion`, render the v1 HTML receipt (fully printed, static). With no JS, the HTML receipt is the page.
- **Bundle budget:** three is loaded only on `/`, and only via dynamic import. Measured three core plus our code is targeted at ≤ 180 kB gzip. State the measured number.

## Motion elsewhere
- **Section choreography** with GSAP: pinned "Method" figures that step through Read → Price → Attest as you scroll (one pinned stage, not three cards). Big numbers count up only where they are not LCP.
- **Framer Motion** for all component interaction: magnetic hover on primary CTAs, springs within 200 ms, and layout transitions in the app tabs.
- **Same as v1:** transform and opacity only; reduced motion turns it all off.

## Speed
"Slow" was observed on the dev server, which compiles each route on first request. Production is what we judge. Targets on a production build:
- LCP < 2.0 s
- CLS < 0.05
- TBT < 200 ms on `/`, measured with Lighthouse

Fonts are subset by next/font, and no web font is render-blocking.

# v3 refinements: inspiration from 21st.dev, Mobbin and Motion (14 Oct 2026)

Sources were researched in the browser with forge. These are principles and mechanisms, not copied code. Each finding is translated into the two-ink receipt language. v3 refines v2; it is not a restart.

## Principles adopted
1. **Verdict before evidence.** Interpret first, then show the data (Mobbin dashboard study: Amplitude, Binance).
2. **Numbers are typography.** Use tabular numerals, per-digit or per-frame spring updates written straight to the DOM, and an **overdamped** spring (no overshoot) for money. Sources: 21st.dev Sliding Number (stiffness 280, damping 18, mass 0.3) and Magic UI Number Ticker (damping 60, stiffness 100). Motion's free recipe: `useMotionValue` → `useTransform` with `Intl` formatting → `animate()`, rendered as a `motion.span` child. Never use the paid Motion+ `AnimateNumber`.
3. **Animate the material's own behaviour:** print, tear, reprint. No generic cross-fades (21st.dev Receipt Pricing: switching plans reprints the slip; print speed is one tunable number).
4. **One moving highlight** with a shared `layoutId` for tabs and segmented controls (Motion layout animations; motion-primitives Animated Background).
5. **Long jobs show named, truthful stages** with real counts and elapsed time, never a fake percentage (Mobbin progress indicators).
6. **Results are permanent and addressable down to the row** (Stripe `receipt_url`, Vercel `#L6` anchors).
7. **Empty and error states teach, and offer one action** (Mobbin empty states).
8. **Effects use theme-aware ink-on-paper colour pairs, never glows.** Grain stays static: paper, not film. Gate tilt by pointer type and by reduced motion.

## Mapped changes

| Surface | Change | From |

## Anti-patterns confirmed (do not use)
- 21st.dev's glowing search bar, liquid metal hero, background beams and shader backgrounds: dark, neon aesthetics that clash with paper.
- Animated grain jitter.
- Holographic or foil effects.
- Any Motion+ (paid) component.

# v4: the Cost Observatory (14 Oct 2026, replaces v1–v3 where they conflict)

The user's verdict on v3: "too whiteish, a lot of empty space, looks OLD, not futuristic, enthusiastic and interactive". v4 is a full reimagining in the motionui **future** register (skill `motionui:futuristic`, reference build `/Users/okwared/Softwares/my-mods/motionui/skills/futuristic/examples/instrument-hero.html`).

**Seed:** spacecraft glass cockpit, dense point cloud, one emissive signal colour, live idle motion, real post stack. White is rejected, so the field is the brand's deep ink. Borrowed from a second seed: a false-colour ramp that encodes real cost.

## Field, colour, type
- **Field:** dark. `<html data-theme="dark">` is set in the root layout. Tokens are in `src/styles/tokens.css` (regenerated with motionui `system`: accent `#3FBF8F` green-on-ink, tinted neutrals, compact, sharp, light and dark both contrast-checked). Use roles only (`--page`, `--surface`, `--raised`, `--text`, `--accent`, …). The light field still exists (`data-theme="light"`), but dark is the product.
- **Signal colour:** green `--accent`, the only emissive hue in the UI.
- **Cost ramp** (data only, never decoration): cheap is green, middle is amber, expensive is red. The position on the ramp is `log10(medianMicroUsd)`, mapped across the report's own min to max. Every coloured point carries a text label too; colour is never the only signal.
- **Type:**
  - IBM Plex Sans Condensed 600 for display, at huge sizes.
  - Plex Sans for UI.
  - Plex Mono with `tabular-nums` for every readout.
  - No sci-fi fonts.
- **Logo on dark:** `public/brand/centscan-mark-dark.svg` (copy it from `brand/` if it's missing) beside the wordmark set in Plex in `--text`.

## Truth rule for readouts
Every number on screen is real:
- **Live chain:** `GET /api/chain` returns `{ ok, data: { blockNumber, baseFeeGwei, timestamp, transferMicroUsd } }`. It is cached for 2 s server-side; poll it at most every 2 s, and only while the tab is visible.
- **Report data:** `getRecentReports` / `ReportPayload`.
- **Client-measured:** fps, pointer, elapsed time.

No fake "SYSTEM ONLINE", no random hex, no invented throughput. Decorative particles are structure and are never labelled as data.

## Site `/`: five layers in the hero
1. **Scene:** the ¢ mark as a 3D object. The open arc is a partial torus and the bar a capsule, in a physical material: metallic plus a thin-film fresnel tint limited to greens and ice, with `fwidth` contour lines so it reads as scanned. Three tilted orbit rings stand for the 1 h, 24 h and 7 d windows.
2. **Volume:** 8–12k GPU particles stream along spiral paths into the mark, like transactions arriving. Positions are computed in the vertex shader, one draw call, with a manual `boundingSphere`. Dim; additive blending.
3. **Data nodes:** the real functions from the newest cached report. Each is an emissive node orbiting the mark at a radius set by `log(median cost)`, sized by `calls`, coloured from the cost ramp. Each node has a diegetic HTML label projected from 3D, e.g. "aggregate3 · 19 calls · 1.08¢", dimmed when behind the mark. With no report there are no nodes, and a "Scan a contract to light this up" label shows instead.
4. **Light:** `UnrealBloomPass` with threshold about 0.62, so only the nodes, contours and accents bloom. Grain, a mild vignette, and chromatic aberration only during the boot transition.
5. **HUD and motion:**
   - **Telemetry rail:** live block number ticking, base fee in gwei, plain transfer cost in ¢, seconds since the last block, fps.
   - **Pointer and labels:** a reticle following the pointer, rulers and the report readouts.
   - **Boot timeline (GSAP):** particles assemble into the ¢, the readouts decode-scramble to their real values, then live idle (parts at different rates, some still).
   - **Interaction:** pointer parallax; hovering a data node highlights its label and table row.
   - **Scroll:** a camera dolly through the sections.
   - **Pause:** a visible pause control (WCAG 2.2.2).
   - **Reduced motion:** one finished still frame.
- **Hero copy and controls:**
  - The H1 "On Arc, gas is a price." stays plain HTML, never animated (LCP).
  - The **address input lives in the hero**: paste and scan goes straight to `/c/<addr>`, with inline validation and example chips from real cached reports plus Arc USDC `0x3600…0000`.
  - "Launch app" goes to `/app`.

## Site sections (dense, interactive, no empty bands)
- **Live from Arc:** a full-width telemetry strip from `/api/chain`, plus the newest report's verdict sentence.
- **How it measures:** an interactive pipeline diagram (logs → receipts → selectors → cents → hash → Arc). Hovering or focusing a stage lights its path and shows the real numbers from the newest report (pages, transactions, functions).
- **The formula:** live. It uses the current base fee from `/api/chain` to show `21,000 × baseFee = X¢` updating, next to the documented receipt `183,288 × 24.000001249 gwei = 0.440¢`.
- **Proof:** the registry address and how to verify, with a "recompute hash" demo on the newest report (canonicalHash runs in the browser).
- **Badge:** the live badge as a ticket with a subtle pointer tilt, plus copy.
- **Final CTA.**
- **Layout:** fluid 12-column grid to 1600 px, mixing asymmetric spans. No identical card grids, and no section with a big empty half.

## App `/app` and `/c/[address]`: mission console
- **App bar:** logo, Scanner, a live chain ticker (block number and base fee from `/api/chain`) and a link to `/`.
- **`/app`:**
  - A command-bar style address input, big mono, with a placeholder cycling real addresses and example chips.
  - The window as a segmented control.
  - Beside it, a live "recent scans" ledger with cost-ramp swatches and labels.
  - Below, a compact live telemetry panel.
- **`/c/[address]`:**
  - **Header:** the verdict sentence first (deterministic, from report fields). The address resolves from scrambled hex (fresh reports only). Window switch and Refresh.
  - **KPI readouts:** a huge mono median in ¢ (overdamped count-up on fresh reports), calls, unique senders, success rate and coverage.
  - **Cost-range chart (signature of the app):** interactive SVG. One row per function, a bar from min to p90 with a median tick, ramp-coloured, a hover/focus crosshair with a tooltip, keyboard-navigable rows, and clicking a row scrolls to its table row.
  - **Cost table:** row anchors `#fn-<selector>` with "Copy link"; becomes a stacked list below 480 px.
  - **Receipt:** a HUD "printout" panel with the same lines, plus the stamp when attested.
  - **Tabs:** Badge (preview from `/badge/<addr>.svg?hours=N`), Attest, Verify.
- **Build progress:** a live pipeline with named stages, counts, elapsed seconds per stage, and the current stage pulsing in the signal colour.
- **Every PRD §6 state stays:** empty, invalid, loading, success, partial, no-activity, error and rate-limited.

## Motion budget and contract
- **Libraries:** three.js and GSAP on `/` only (dynamic import after first paint, own chunk). Framer Motion in the app.
- **Hero performance:**
  - DPR capped at 1.75, with adaptive quality: watch frame time and drop DPR, then particles.
  - Particles and mesh halved below 720 px.
  - Render only while the hero is visible.
  - Stop on `visibilitychange`, dispose on unmount or `pagehide`.
  - Guard zero-size resizes.
  - No WebGL: show a static, styled HUD with the data nodes as an HTML list.
- **Speed targets (production):** LCP < 2.0 s on throttled mobile, CLS < 0.05, TBT < 200 ms. State gzip sizes.
- **Never:** purple or cyan neon, gradient text, glowing borders on every card, glass over blob gradients, hex grids or matrix rain, fake terminal logs, "Initialising…" gates in front of real content, constant glitch.
